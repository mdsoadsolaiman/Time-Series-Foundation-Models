## Foundational Methodology — Pre-2022

1. Diebold, F. X., & Mariano, R. S. (1995). Comparing predictive accuracy. *Journal of Business & Economic Statistics, 13*(3), 253–263. https://doi.org/10.1080/07350015.1995.10524599  
   **Why retained:** Foundational source for the forecast-comparison test used by the project; not presented as recent literature.
2. Gneiting, T., Balabdaoui, F., & Raftery, A. E. (2007). Probabilistic forecasts, calibration and sharpness. *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B, 69*(2), 243–268. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9868.2007.00587.x  
   **Why retained:** Supplies the foundational distinction between calibration and sharpness used to interpret intervals.
3. Harvey, D., Leybourne, S., & Newbold, P. (1997). Testing the equality of prediction mean squared errors. *International Journal of Forecasting, 13*(2), 281–291. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0169-2070(96)00719-4  
   **Why retained:** Provides essential small-sample context for Diebold–Mariano-style inference.
4. Stankevičiūtė, K., Alaa, A. M., & van der Schaar, M. (2021). Conformal time-series forecasting. *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems, 34*, 6216–6228. https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/hash/312f1ba2a72318edaaa995a67835fad5-Abstract.html  
   **Why retained:** Establishes multi-horizon conformal forecasting concepts needed for the proposed leakage-free calibration extension.
